## What VOXBULK is

VOXBULK is a UK-built AI platform for businesses that need to screen candidates, run WhatsApp and phone surveys, capture QR customer feedback, and collect exhibition leads — then act from live dashboards.

## Who it is for

- Recruitment and HR teams (AI voice interview screening)
- Dental, medical, opticians, beauty, wellness and multi-location operators (recall, feedback, surveys)
- Operations and CX teams that need high-response WhatsApp surveys
- Exhibitors and event teams (Expo booth lead capture)
- Any business that makes outbound calls or WhatsApp messages at scale

## Active products (live now)

### 1. AI Interview Screening (Recruitment Automation)
- CV intake and ATS-style scoring against the role
- WhatsApp / email booking links for candidates
- 10–12 minute AI voice interviews with ranked scorecards, transcripts and hire recommendations
- Optional calendar / ATS integrations (e.g. Zoho Recruit, Cronofy / Calendly where configured)
- Dashboard: campaigns, results, shortlist, final-round booking

### 2. WhatsApp Surveys
- Conversational surveys inside WhatsApp (approved templates)
- Linear or branching question flows
- High open rates vs email; multilingual replies with English dashboard summaries
- Results, NPS-style indicators and export in the client dashboard

### 3. AI Calling Surveys
- Outbound AI phone surveys that ask questions aloud and capture spoken answers
- Calling windows and compliance-aware dialling
- Best for senior cohorts, recall outreach, or lists that prefer voice over chat

### 4. Customer Feedback (QR)
- One QR per branch / location → WhatsApp in ~30 seconds
- Voice notes in 50+ languages with English dashboard translation
- Topic ratings, red-flag recovery, multi-location compare
- Printable QR materials; edit topics without reprinting the code

### 5. VoxBulk Expo
- Exhibition booth lead capture on WhatsApp
- Unique QR per exhibitor; qualifying questions; optional brochure delivery
- Hot / Warm / Cold lead scoring and CSV export
- One-off duration packages (e.g. 1 / 3 / 7 day booth windows) — not a monthly subscription

### 6. Platform / account
- Multi-currency billing (GBP, AUD, CAD, USD) with packages and pay-as-you-go wallet
- Team invites and organisation roles
- GDPR-oriented UK / EU data practices; calls can be recorded for quality where disclosed
